## Who We Are
Craftly Studio engineers custom, high-performance web platforms, bespoke web applications, enterprise dashboards, and AI automation solutions for businesses across Gujarat and India.

## Core Philosophy
Most digital agencies build on slow, bloated templates with dozens of fragile third-party plugins. Craftly Studio hand-codes modern digital experiences using Next.js 16, React 19, strict TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. The result is websites that load under 1.5 seconds, achieve 90+ Lighthouse scores, and convert visitors into qualified business leads.
